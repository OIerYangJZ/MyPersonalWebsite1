Company

Research depth with an engineering endpoint.

Jinsey Lab was founded in 2026 to work on technical problems where research quality and software quality need to coexist.

We focus on problems that benefit from careful reasoning, explicit evaluation, and implementation that can be inspected rather than treated as a black box. Current work spans agentic software systems, quantum compilation, and tools for research-intensive workflows.
