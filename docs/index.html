Company

Built around hard technical problems.

Jinsey Lab is an independent technology company founded in 2026. We combine research, systems engineering, and open technical work to develop software that is rigorous enough for research and practical enough to use.

Our work spans agentic software engineering, quantum compilation, and research tooling. We favor inspectable systems, reproducible results, and engineering decisions that can be defended with evidence.
